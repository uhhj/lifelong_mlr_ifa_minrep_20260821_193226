\section{Conclusion}
RA-Coreset organizes representative demonstration replay with protected random
anchors, while SHBR carries autonomous success histories into later learning
through fixed behavior references and controlled updates. Experiments on two
LIBERO suites support gains in mean learning-curve and final-task performance
over the reported controls.
