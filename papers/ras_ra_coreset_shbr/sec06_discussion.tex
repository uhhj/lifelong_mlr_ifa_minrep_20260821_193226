\section{Discussion}
Demonstration replay preserves supervision for learning and correction,
while success histories retain references encountered during autonomous
completion. RA-Coreset changes replay organization; SHBR changes histories
and update decisions used for retention. Aggregate improvements coexist
with negative task cells and seed-dependent outcomes. Low retention loss
on stored histories is not itself evidence of closed-loop improvement.
The study covers simulation and development seeds. Additional interaction,
memory, and candidate computation are reported explicitly.
