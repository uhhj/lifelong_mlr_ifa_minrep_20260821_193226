\subsection{Random-anchored coreset replay}
Let $U$ be candidate demonstration windows, $b=\lceil|U|/2\rceil$ the quota,
and $S_0$ the original size-$b$ greedy $k$-center set. Reproduce the same-seed
ordered random sample of size $b$ and protect its first $\lfloor b/2\rfloor$
members as anchors $A$. Starting from $S_0$, insert missing anchors in
their random order. After each insertion remove a non-anchor to minimize
\begin{equation}r(S)=\max_{u\in U}\min_{v\in S}d(u,v).\end{equation}
Ties use mean nearest distance, then deterministic identity order.
Exactly $|A\setminus S_0|$ exchanges preserve $|S|=b$ and ensure $A\subseteq S$.
Window payloads and calibrated distance are retained; old sets are not reselected.

\subsection{Demonstration-learning objective}
The strong control $C$ includes continuous expert supervision.
For expert action $y_i$, define
\begin{align}
c_{im}&=\sum_g\frac{\|\mu_{im,g}-y_{i,g}\|^2+\|\sigma_{im,g}\|^2}{|g|s_g^2},\\
r(z,j)&=\left[\delta+\max_{m\ne j}z_m-z_j\right]_+ .
\end{align}
Groups $g$ are translation, rotation, and gripper coordinates;
$s_g$ is frozen training-action RMS with floor $0.1$.
With $j_i^E=\arg\min_m c_{im}$, the objective is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:control}
L_C=L_{\mathrm{BC}}+0.1L_{\mathrm{IFA}}+
0.1\E_{i\in\mathrm{old,valid}}[c_{i,k_i}+0.1r(z_i,j_i^E)].
\end{equation}
Expert auxiliary terms apply to valid old-task windows and are retained in both branches.
\subsection{Success histories and fixed references}
After task acquisition, ten training rollouts use initial states separate
from formal evaluation. Fixed trajectory quotas and reservoir sampling retain
up to 128 histories per task from successful trajectories. Unused quotas are
not filled by extra rollouts or redistribution. Each record contains latent
history and the acquisition policy's executed Gaussian $(\mu_i^T,\sigma_i^T)$.
This distribution reference is fixed in subsequent stages. For $n_t^+$
distinct represented successful trajectories, define
\begin{equation}q_t=n_t^+/10.\end{equation}
This is trajectory support, not a calibrated action-correctness probability.
\subsection{Execution retention and routing}
Define the grouped diagonal-Gaussian Wasserstein distance
\begin{equation}
d_{im}=\sum_g\frac{\|\mu_{im,g}-\mu_{i,g}^T\|^2+
\|\sigma_{im,g}-\sigma_{i,g}^T\|^2}{|g|s_g^2}.
\end{equation}
The executed component receives regression; the route target
$j_i^T=\arg\min_m d_{im}$ favors the closest component without tying
component indices across stages. Four task-balanced rotating slots give
\begin{align}
L_{\mathrm{ret}}&=\frac14\sum_{(t,i)\in\mathcal B_{\mathrm{succ}}}
q_t[d_{i,k_i}+0.1r(z_i,j_i^T)],\\
L_Q&=L_C+\lambda_RL_{\mathrm{ret}} .
\end{align}
Empty slots contribute zero; the denominator remains four.
The primary setting uses $\lambda_R=0.1$ and $\delta=0.1$.
\subsection{Controlled update selection}
Each block of ten updates (or shorter epoch tail) produces candidates $C$
and $Q$ from the same full training state and mini-batches. Fixed training
probes measure current BC $B$, execution cost $E$, and support-weighted
old-behavior retention $D$. Select $Q$ exactly when
\begin{equation}
B(Q)\le B(C),\qquad E(Q)\le E(C),\qquad D(Q)<D(C);
\end{equation}
otherwise select $C$. Restore the chosen model, optimizer, scheduler,
gradients, modes, RNG, and cursor together. Probes contain up to 64 current
valid windows and eight histories per old task. The old-task criterion
is aggregated, not a guarantee for every task or formal rollout.
Deployment keeps the original architecture and action sampler.
