\title{Continual Robot Imitation Learning with Random-Anchored Replay
and Success-History Behavior Retention}
\author{Author names to be supplied}
\affiliation{organization={Affiliation to be supplied}}
\begin{abstract}
Continual robot imitation learning requires a shared policy to acquire new
skills while retaining previously acquired behavior. Limited replay memory
restricts the demonstration experience available during later learning,
while autonomous execution provides additional histories on which the robot
has already completed a task. We combine two complementary mechanisms.
Random-Anchored Coreset replay (RA-Coreset) incorporates protected random
anchors into representative demonstration selection at a fixed replay quota.
Success-History Behavior Retention (SHBR) stores successful autonomous
histories with fixed execution-distribution references. It weights retention
by trajectory support and selects between matched candidate updates using
training-only criteria for current-task learning and old-behavior retention.
The framework builds on multimodal latent replay and incremental feature
adjustment without changing the deployed policy architecture. Across three
seeds on LIBERO-GOAL and LIBERO-OBJECT, the complete method achieves mean
AUCs of 87.26\% and 86.49\%, respectively. Comparisons with an
optimizer-call-matched control and an ungated retention variant examine
the value and computational cost of the design.
\end{abstract}
