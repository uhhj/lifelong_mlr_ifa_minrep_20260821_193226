\section{Problem setting}
Tasks arrive sequentially with demonstration set $\mathcal D_t$ for task
$t$. A shared policy $\pi_\theta(a\mid h,\ell)$ conditions on observation
history $h$ and language instruction $\ell$. Historical demonstrations
are retained in $\mathcal M^{\mathrm{demo}}$; a separate memory
$\mathcal M^{\mathrm{succ}}$ stores successful autonomous histories.
Its storage and interaction costs are reported separately.
The policy predicts $K=5$ Gaussian components
$\{(\mu_m,\sigma_m,z_m)\}_{m=1}^K$. Deployment selects
\begin{equation}
k=\arg\max_m z_m,\qquad
a\sim\mathcal N(\mu_k,\operatorname{diag}(\sigma_k^2)).
\end{equation}
The mixture-based behavior-cloning loss and deployed component selection
are distinguished explicitly.
We retain the MLR+IFA backbone, temporal processing, and valid
observation--next-action alignment (ALIGN/TASA in experiment records).
