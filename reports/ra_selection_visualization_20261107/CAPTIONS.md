# Publication captions

**Figure 4. Selection membership and demonstration allocation of RA-Coreset.** Panels (a,c) show the K-Center initialization, protected random anchors, and final RA-Coreset for the first seed and task (seed 41, Task 6) in OBJECT and GOAL. The horizontal coordinate is the window's target timestep divided by trajectory length minus one; the vertical coordinate identifies its demonstration. Gray points show the candidate population; colored points show membership, red crosses mark removed K-Center windows, and orange ticks mark inserted anchors. All panels within a suite use the same candidate population and coordinates. Panels (b,d) report the percentage of candidate windows retained from each demonstration by full-quota Random, K-Center, and RA-Coreset. K-Center and RA retain 781 of 1,562 windows in OBJECT and 499 of 997 in GOAL; the anchor subsets contain 390 and 249 windows, respectively, and are not the full-quota Random comparator. RA performs 199 and 119 exchanges while retaining every protected anchor. Four OBJECT candidate windows have ambiguous demonstration identity and are not positioned or assigned to a demonstration; their method-specific counts are disclosed in the figure. Window multiplicities are counted even when plotted points coincide. This is a selection-membership visualization, not a latent-space projection or an evaluation of policy success.

**Figure S3. Original-distance trade-offs after anchor-preserving exchanges.** Ratios of the mean (a,c) and maximum (b,d) nearest-selected-window distances for RA-Coreset relative to its K-Center initialization across seeds 41–43 and Tasks 6–9. Distances are archived values from the actual selection-time visual–action metric; no two-dimensional projection distance is used. The denominator and query population are identical within each paired case. Dashed lines indicate equality; values below one indicate lower distances. RA preserves all prescribed anchors at the original quota, with mean-distance changes between −1.25% and +1.87% and coverage-radius increases between 2.93% and 7.79% across the 24 cases. Task 9 memory is constructed after the final training stage and is not replayed later in this four-stage experiment. Points show separate cases; lines only connect tasks within a seed and do not represent confidence intervals.

Suggested main-text wording:

“Figure 4 makes the anchor-preserving exchange process explicit: RA-Coreset retains the full prescribed anchor subset while maintaining the original replay quota. In the illustrated cases, all three full-quota selectors represent the ten demonstrations, but allocate their retained windows differently across demonstrations and time. The complete selection-distance analysis (Fig. S3) characterizes the associated geometric trade-off: relative to K-Center, mean nearest-center distances change by less than 1.9% in magnitude, whereas maximum distances increase by 2.9–7.8%. These results describe anchor retention and coverage trade-offs rather than a universal reduction in K-Center radius.”
