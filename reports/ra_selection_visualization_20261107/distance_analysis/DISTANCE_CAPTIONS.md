# Captions and results paragraph

**Figure 4. RA-Coreset selections and original-distance coverage.** For OBJECT (a,b) and GOAL (c,d), the left panels display the K-Center initialization, protected random anchors, and final RA-Coreset using one shared t-SNE projection of the full candidate population (seed 41, Task 6). Gray points are candidates and colored points indicate subset membership. The anchor subset contains half of the replay quota and is not the full-quota Random comparator. The right panels show empirical cumulative distributions of nearest-selected-window distances for Random, K-Center, and RA-Coreset at identical quotas and on the same full query population. These distances are recomputed using the frozen original visual–action metric, not the two-dimensional projection. Selected windows remain in the query population, producing the approximately 50% mass at zero. A higher CDF at a positive distance indicates that more candidate windows lie within that distance of the selected set. The projection is illustrative and cannot establish coverage quality. All 24 suite/seed/task cases are provided in Figs. S4–S5.

**Figure S4. Original-distance ECDFs for all OBJECT selection cases.** Rows correspond to Tasks 6–9 and columns to seeds 41–43. Each panel compares full-quota Random, K-Center, and RA-Coreset on the same full candidate population, including selected-window zero self-distances. Distances use the original frozen visual–action metric. Task 9 storage occurs after final-stage training and is not subsequently replayed in this experiment. Curves are descriptive, without window-level independence assumptions or confidence intervals.

**Figure S5. Original-distance ECDFs for all GOAL selection cases.** Layout and distance definitions follow Fig. S4. No case is omitted or selected according to its outcome.

Suggested results paragraph:

“RA-Coreset preserves every prescribed random anchor while retaining geometry close to the K-Center initialization. Across the 12 seed–task cases per suite, its mean nearest-center distance is on average 0.79% higher than K-Center on OBJECT and 0.61% higher on GOAL, with average coverage-radius increases of 4.71% on both suites. Relative to full-quota Random selection, RA reduces mean distances by 13.95% and 13.53%, the 95th-percentile distances by 26.56% and 24.68%, and maximum distances by 52.94% and 48.75% on OBJECT and GOAL, respectively. Each percentage is the unweighted mean of within-case relative changes. The results characterize the geometric trade-off of retaining randomized anchors; downstream policy benefits are assessed separately by the closed-loop experiments.”
