# CAET-v2 implementation details fixed before GPU admission

Gate1 passes. Task7 fully falls back to RA/IFA. Task8/9 graph support is necessary, not sufficient: their fitted predictor must independently pass the frozen heldout tests before either transfer or retention activates.

Auxiliary fit batches use 32 unique anchors from a seeded per-epoch permutation, each optionally paired with one qualified graph neighbor (cyclic epoch index into the fixed neighbor list). Thus actual batch size is at most64, every training point is an anchor exactly once per epoch, paired exposures are separately counted, and no holdout sample is fitted. The physical regression loss is SmoothL1 on training-only centered/scaled effects, plus0.1 mean positive cosine-distance on these actual graph pairs. No task weighting or BC resampling. Fixed action permutation is a one-position cyclic shift of the ordered heldout next actions.

Policy transfer gradients and current-sample BC gradients are computed with an auxiliary RNG context; the native BC+IFA forward and dropout RNG remain untouched. Additional per-step forwards and gradient computation costs are recorded in epoch time. Conflict is a strictly negative FP64 accumulated dot product; it disables only transfer. Retention uses deterministic eval-mode native full-GMM next-action likelihood, with original variance (low_eval_noise=False); full-group epoch scoring and historical-reference scoring use the same mode. The mini-batch retention hinge is a stochastic group estimate; its weights update only on exact full-group scores at epoch boundaries. It is not a hard per-update or closed-loop guarantee. All valid retained samples, including whole-demo predictor holdout samples, may be behavior-protected; holdout independence pertains to effect-predictor fitting only.

Gate2 uses exactly8 isolated optimizer calls: four native/off/full-state-recovery comparisons; one gradient-ascent construction of old next-action likelihood harm and one finite nonzero protection response; two BC plasticity checks with deliberately opposing/concordant additional gradients. The last four use normalized-step SGD on the native policy solely as functional probes, not as formal training. The formal optimizer, learning-rate schedule and updates remain unchanged. No environment calls.

Old CAET-v1 reports remain separate. COVR remains cancelled. LIBERO50 is pending and is not launched by this new finite supervisor. No automatic seed expansion.
